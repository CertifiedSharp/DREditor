# Jargon
In DREditor, there are many common terms and phrases used to describe specific systems, functionalities, etc. This page is to compile a glossary of these terms in the event they are not explained in a tutorial or any other do
## Common Jargon used with DREditor
- **Actuator**: Stemming from the definition of a mechanical device that moves or controls or _starts_ something. Actuators are apart of the Trigger system, where they are categorized as the scripts that decide _"How a trigger can be activated, or start."_ . There will only ever be one Actuator per Game Object.
- **Subsequent**: The second half of the trigger system, where they are categorized as the scripts that decide _"What a Trigger should do once it's been activated."_ or _"The subsequent action after actuation."_ . There is no limit to the amount of Subsequents per game object.
- **NSD**: Non-Stop Debate.
- **Panel** (Debate Context): A panel is best understood as a Dialogue line that additionally contains data for phrases. This "Dialogue line" is broken down into pieces at the discretion of the user, these pieces become phrases.
- **Phrase** (Debate Context): Phrases are the word or words broken down from panels, each phrase has the ability to have dynamic data applied to it and can then be animated at the discretion of the user. This functionality is the foundation for debate style gameplay. 
- **RTMM**: Return to Main Menu.
- **TPFD**: Two-Point-Five D or (2.5D). Relating to the cardboard cutout style of how rooms can be viewed through DREditor.
- **FTE**: Free-Time Event, or time during the gameplay process that can be treated as a "Free-Roaming" mode.
